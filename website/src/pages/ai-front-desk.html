Telecoms and IT support

A support playbook out of the box

Built for first-line technical support, so your partners' customers get a useful answer, not a voicemail.

  • Fault types: no service, can't call out, can't reach mobiles or abroad, one-way or poor audio.
  • Priority rules: whole-site outages go straight to an engineer with a High priority case.
  • Asks before touching an existing case, so unrelated issues never get mixed up.
  • Your own wording, opening hours and escalation rules in plain text.
Playbook · excerpt
PRIORITY FOR THE CASE
- High: the whole office or site cannot make or receive calls.
- Medium: one user, or calls to some destinations fail.
- Low: questions, feature requests, one-off issues.

AFTER THE CASE
- Read the case number to the caller digit by digit.
- High priority: transfer to an engineer now, with a summary.
