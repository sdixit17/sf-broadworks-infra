FAQ

Questions partners ask

Do my customers need to change their phone system?

No. {{brand}} runs on enterprise UC, so calls keep ringing on the desk phones and Webex app your customers already use. The AI and CRM features are added on top.

Can the AI really raise and update tickets?

Yes. It finds the caller by phone number, reads their open cases, adds notes and creates new cases with a priority, in Salesforce or HubSpot. You decide whether it may write or only read.

What happens when the AI can't help?

It hands the caller to your team's queue. The agent sees the AI's summary and the whole conversation on screen as the call arrives, so the caller never has to repeat themselves.

Is it really white label?

Yes. The greeting, the company name the AI uses, the reports and the portal carry your brand. Your customers buy from you.

How quickly can we launch?

A first customer can be live in days: connect the number, load your support playbook and choose where calls go when a person is needed.
