Accessibility

Last updated 10 November 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use telqon.com. The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Clear heading structure and a "skip to content" link on every page.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Colours chosen for contrast in both light and dark mode.
  • Text alternatives for diagrams; animation is turned off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages resize to phones and to 200% zoom without losing content.

Found a problem?

Write to info@telqon.com with the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within five working days.
