Last updated 10 November 2026.

We don't sell personal information or use it for automated decisions about you.

Only the providers that run the site and our email and customer records for us (for example our hosting and CRM providers), under contracts that protect your data. We may also share information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer, in which case our customer agreement applies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Write to info@telqon.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
