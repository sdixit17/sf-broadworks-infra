About

Telecom people who write software

Telqon brings together two skills that rarely sit in one team: running carrier voice platforms, and building modern software on top of them.

Why we exist

Service providers carry the calls, own the numbers and know their business customers. Yet when those customers want calling inside their CRM, a contact centre or an AI receptionist, they are pushed towards Microsoft Teams, Zoom and the other cloud platforms.

The gap is rarely the network. It is the software around it, and the people to build and run it. Telqon closes that gap: we manage the platform and build the features, so a provider can offer a complete cloud communications service under its own brand.

How we work

  • Engineers talk to engineers. You speak to the people doing the work.
  • Everything is written down: designs, test plans, runbooks and gap lists.
  • We test on simulated platforms first, then on yours, before anything goes live.
  • Software is multi-tenant, white-label and built to fail safely: calls stay on your network if our layer stops.
  • Open about limits. If a platform can't do something, we say so and suggest the alternative.
